{# ============================== DON'T WASTE MONEY ============================== #}

Save money

Don't waste money on AI

One of the biggest mistakes businesses make with AI is overpaying for it. The big brands have the best models, but they are a huge waste of money.

The big brands

Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's GPT are world-class models — and priced that way. For reading paperwork, summarizing records, and drafting documents, you're often paying for far more model than the job needs.

Open-weights models

DeepSeek, Alibaba's Qwen, Moonshot's Kimi, Z.ai's GLM, and Xiaomi's MiMo — the leading open-weights models — do the same everyday work at a fraction of the cost, often an order of magnitude less. The best of them, like Qwen 3.8 and Kimi K3, now rival the premium brands on everyday tasks. For most back-office tasks, you won't notice any difference in the results.

Three rules to avoid wasting money

Start small

Begin with an affordable model. Move to a premium one only if it genuinely fails at the job.

Match the model to the task

A 30-second job doesn't need a frontier model. Lower-cost open models handle most back-office work fine.

Don't buy the hype

Beware of expensive "AI-powered" subscriptions — a small custom tool often costs less and fits better.

That's the kind of straight answer you'll get over coffee — which model to use, what it actually costs, and where the money is being wasted.
